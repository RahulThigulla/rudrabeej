RUDRABEEJ — 1 TO 21 MUKHI IMAGE ASSETS FOLDER
==============================================================

To replace the images of your 1 to 21 Mukhi Rudraksha beads with your own photos:

1. Copy your photos into this folder:
   c:\Users\Lenovo\OneDrive\Desktop\my website\public\images\rudraksha\

   - 9-mukhi.jpg   (for 9 Mukhi / Nava Mukhi)
   - 10-mukhi.jpg  (for 10 Mukhi / Dasha Mukhi)

   - 14-mukhi.jpg  (for 14 Mukhi / Chaturdasha Mukhi)
   - 15-mukhi.jpg  (for 15 Mukhi / 15 Mukhi Pashupatinath)
